Teaching the Kentucky Graduate Profile

The Kentucky Graduate Profile names 10 Essential Skills that every graduate of a Kentucky public college or university should develop. This guide collects resources for teaching each one, organized by skill number. Skill 8 is ready now; the others are on the way.
