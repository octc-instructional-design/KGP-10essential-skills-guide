8.1 Contributing to Team Goals

Indicator 8.1 of Essential Skill 8: Collaboration and Teamwork

From Benchmark to Milestone

BenchmarkCollege entry

Supports team decisions by participating in discussions.

→

Milestone 8.1End of two years of college

Contributes ideas aligned to identified objectives of the team.

I Can Statements

  • I can share ideas that are relevant to what my team is trying to accomplish.
  • I can participate in team discussions in a way that moves the group toward its goal.
  • I can connect my contributions to the team's objectives rather than just my own perspective.

What to Look For

A student working at the Milestone level:

  • Offers at least one idea or suggestion during team discussions that directly relates to the team's stated goal
  • Can articulate how their contribution connects to the team's objectives when asked
  • Participates actively rather than deferring all decisions to other team members
  • Builds on others' ideas rather than redirecting the conversation away from team goals

Assessment Ideas by Discipline

Technical & Trades

  • In-PersonDuring a team project or lab, observe whether each student contributes at least one relevant idea during planning or problem-solving — simple observation checklist
  • Online / BlackboardUse a Group Discussion where each student must post a specific idea tied to the team's stated objective before the group moves forward
  • Lab / HybridUse Peer Review at the end of a team project — peers assess whether each member's contributions were relevant and goal-directed

Health Sciences

  • In-PersonDuring a care planning or case study team activity, assess whether each student's suggestions align with the patient's identified care goals
  • Online / BlackboardAssign a Group Discussion around a case scenario; each student must contribute a care recommendation tied to identified patient outcomes
  • Lab / HybridUse a structured team simulation where roles are assigned and each member must verbally contribute a relevant idea before the group proceeds

General Education & Business

  • In-PersonDuring group projects or team presentations, track whether individual contributions are aligned to the stated project objectives — peer and instructor observation
  • Online / BlackboardUse Blackboard Groups with a collaborative Discussion; require each student to post a goal-aligned contribution and respond to at least one peer
  • HybridAssign a team planning document where each member's contributions are visible and tied to shared objectives — assessable for alignment and relevance

Practical Tips for Faculty

  • Make team objectives explicit and visible before any group activity — students can't contribute toward a goal they haven't clearly identified
  • A simple contribution log (who said what, how it connected to the goal) gives you something concrete to assess during or after group work
  • Peer assessment at the end of a team activity is one of the most efficient ways to surface whether contributions were meaningful — build it into the assignment, not as an afterthought
  • In online courses, threaded group discussions make individual contributions visible in a way that in-person group work often doesn't
