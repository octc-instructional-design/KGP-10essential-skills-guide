Collaboration and Teamwork

Collaborate and work in teams

Definition: Graduates will collaborate with colleagues, become effective team members, and manage conflict.

Using this guide

Each Essential Skill has a statewide rubric that describes growth at three levels. By the end of two years of college, students should be working at the Milestone level, so that column is the focus here.

The rubric for this skill has 4 rows. Each row has its own page that breaks the Milestone into behaviors you can observe, with assessment ideas for different disciplines and settings. If your course is mapped to this skill, start with the row that fits the assignment you have in mind.

The Skill 8 Rubric

Indicator BenchmarkCollege entry MilestoneEnd of two years of college CapstoneBachelor's program completion
8.1Contributing to Team Goals Supports team decisions by participating in discussions. Contributes ideas aligned to identified objectives of the team. Advocates for evidence-based solutions that promote the teams' identified objectives.
8.2Professional Communication & Feedback Responds promptly to messages and requests for information. Addresses conflicts constructively, communicating expectations and adjusting own behavior based on feedback received. Fosters strong team cohesion by giving and soliciting feedback and responding transparently.
8.3Roles & Responsibilities Completes tasks on time and recognizes how individual responsibilities impact team goals. Identifies tasks required to reach a goal and learns about team members' assets to support the distribution of roles and responsibilities. Collaborates with multiple teams, utilizing different perspectives and skills to achieve common goals.
8.4Adaptability & Trust Acknowledges both strengths and limitations within a team. Adapts to changing project requirements and roles in a way that fosters mutual trust and respect amongst team members. Navigates between leading and supporting through advocacy for mutual goals and integration of team members' strengths.

Source: Kentucky Graduate Profile Levels of Mastery rubric, Essential Skill 8 (CPE, 9/15/2025). Indicator names are OCTC's.
